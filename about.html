About The Anchor

The Anchor is Morse High School's student newspaper. Students write, edit and publish every edition.

We cover news, sports, clubs, the arts and pop culture at Morse, and we're starting a video podcast. If you have an idea for a story, send it to us on the Pitch a Story page.
